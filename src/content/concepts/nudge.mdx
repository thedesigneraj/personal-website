## The problem

People set personal goals but struggle to stay consistent and motivated, so most habits fail and long-term change doesn't stick.

Most self-help and productivity apps offer static checklists or generic reminders. They don't adapt to a person's needs, emotional state or behaviour patterns. That lack of personal, well-timed support creates a motivation gap, especially when someone hits a rough patch or loses momentum.

## The idea

Use AI grounded in behavioural science to deliver personalised prompts, motivational nudges and practical tips at the right moment. By learning from each person's behaviour and progress, Nudge becomes a support system that keeps them engaged, accountable and on track.

## Design decisions

- **Minimal, distraction-free.** A clean interface that keeps the focus on your goals and prompts. Nothing you don't need.
- **Personalised dashboards.** The home screen adapts to your progress, with tailored insights, streaks and upcoming nudges.
- **Micro-interactions that matter.** Animations, subtle haptics and encouraging visuals make small wins feel rewarding without being overwhelming.
- **Emotionally intelligent flows.** Nudge pushes gently, without guilt. Whether you're crushing it or slacking, it meets you with empathy, not pressure.
