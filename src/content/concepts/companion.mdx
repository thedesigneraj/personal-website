## The problem

People want to do everyday things with someone, but there's no easy, casual way to find a companion.

Most social apps are built for performance (followers, dating, curated profiles) or need rigid planning. They don't support spontaneous, low-pressure meetups. So people end up doing things alone, even when they'd rather not. The emotional cost is isolation. The practical cost is missed chances to connect, explore and build community.

## The idea

A simple, approachable platform where you can match with people nearby who are up for the same activity at the same time. No swiping. No small-talk pressure. Just shared intent and a quick connection.

## Design goals

- Feel calm, casual and non-judgemental.
- Make it easy to find people by activity and availability.
- Put shared interest and real-time connection ahead of profiles.
- Reduce friction in starting a conversation or making a plan.
- Avoid the dating-app feel: more hangout, less hookup.

## Visual decisions

- A muted palette, so it never feels hyper-social or high-alert.
- Rounded cards and soft shadows to keep the tone friendly.
- Typography-led hierarchy for quick scanning.
- Icons and avatars do more work than long bios.
- Plenty of whitespace to cut clutter and stress.
