## The problem

The client's HR team was doing too much of hiring by hand: posting roles, sorting through applications, deciding who to shortlist, and sending assessments. They needed one place to run recruitment, and candidates needed a clear way to apply and follow their application.

## My role

It was a contract role, and I was the only designer, working with three developers. I worked directly with the client: I spoke with their HR teams to understand their problems, presented designs, and iterated with them until the flows worked for the people who'd use them every day.

## Key decisions

### Let the system do the sorting

I designed the Recruitment CRM around automated résumé parsing and candidate scoring, with bulk actions and smart filters, so HR spends less time on manual sorting and more on the people worth talking to.

### One flow from job post to shortlist

Post a job, review candidates, shortlist the best ones, and send them assessments and tests, all in one continuous flow instead of separate tools.

### An AI assistant for HR

An AI chatbot sits inside the portal to make day-to-day tasks easier for the HR team.

### Candidates on any device

On the candidate side I designed online assessments, AI-analysed video applications, profile completion and application tracking, all working across devices.
